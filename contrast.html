Reference

How Scores Map

  1. WCAG normal text passes at 4.5:1 or higher.
  2. WCAG large text and graphical elements pass at 3:1 or higher.
  3. APCA Strong starts at absolute Lc 75.
  4. APCA Moderate is absolute Lc 45–74.
  5. APCA Weak is absolute Lc < 45.
